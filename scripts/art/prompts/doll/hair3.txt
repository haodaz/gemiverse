Write a new ES module for the doll's hair in the style of images 1 and 2: few, large, soft, rounded volumes, like modelling clay.

export function buildHair(THREE, opts, kit) -> THREE.Group added to the rig's "head" pivot. The head is a smooth near-sphere 0.36 wide, 0.34 tall, 0.33 deep whose centre is 0.165 above the pivot (face toward +z); the eyes are low on the face at 0.115 above the pivot, so the forehead is tall. Ears are at the sides, low. Shoulders are 0.075 below the pivot and 0.085 to each side.
opts = { hairStyle: "longStraight" | "longWavy" | "bob" | "shortLayered" | "pixie" | "ponytail" | "twinTails" | "bun" | "curlyMedium" | "buzz", hairColor, highlight, fringe: "full" | "sideSwept" | "curtain" | "none" }.

Method:
- HAIR CAP: the head ellipsoid scaled 1.08 (0.014 of thickness), covering everything above a hairline that runs across the forehead at 0.235 above the pivot, drops in front of the ears, passes behind them and meets at the nape. Smooth, 64 x 48 segments, the lower edge rounded inward so it looks like a thick shell, not a paper edge.
- LOCKS: each lock is a plump, smooth, tapered volume: a tube built ring by ring along a CatmullRomCurve3 with an elliptical cross-section (about 0.05 to 0.09 wide, 0.025 to 0.04 thick) that swells slightly in the middle and tapers to a ROUNDED point; 16 segments around, 24 along; normals smooth. Locks overlap each other and sink into the cap by a third of their thickness so everything fuses into one soft mass.
- FRINGE: 4 to 6 locks starting at the crown-front, sweeping down and sideways across the forehead in gentle S curves, ending between 0.19 and 0.215 above the pivot (well above the eyes, never over them). "sideSwept" sweeps all one way; "curtain" parts in the middle; "full" is a row of soft points.
- SIDES AND BACK by style: bob (locks to the jaw that curl outward at the tips), longStraight (broad smooth panels down the back to the waist, two locks in front of the shoulders), longWavy (the same with big lazy waves), shortLayered (short overlapping tufts, a little messy), pixie, ponytail (a thick tail from the upper back with a band), twinTails (two high tails that fall and curl up at the ends), bun (a round bun with a wrapped band), curlyMedium (plump spiral curls around the head, each a fat helix), buzz (only the cap, slightly thicker).
- One small antenna curl at the crown for all styles except buzz, bun and ponytail.
- Keep every point of every lock outside the head ellipsoid scaled 1.02 and outside the neck and shoulder volume (a capsule from (-0.105, -0.07, 0) to (0.105, -0.07, 0), radius 0.045).
- Material: one MeshPhysicalMaterial, color opts.hairColor, roughness 0.5, sheen 1, sheenColor opts.highlight, clearcoat 0.15. No textures needed; if you paint one, only a very soft broad highlight band.
export function sway(group, t, amount): each lock rotates a little about its root (2 to 4 degrees, tips most) with its own phase.
