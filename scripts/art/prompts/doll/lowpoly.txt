Write a three.js ES module that builds a LOW-POLY CHIBI character in the style of a faceted paper-craft or low-poly game figure: big head, tiny body, every surface made of a few large flat facets with crisp edges, flat shading, a simple flat-painted anime face. - Body: a small faceted torso 0.20 wide at the shoulders tapering to 0.16 at the waist, 0.24 tall; short faceted arms that taper to a point-like mitten hand; short legs as tapered prisms ending in simple wedge shoes.
- Skeleton, so a shared motion library can animate it. Build this exact hierarchy of THREE.Group pivots, each with its origin at its joint, and attach meshes to them:

  "pelvis" > "legL" (0.055, 0, 0) > "shinL" (0, -0.13, 0) > "footL" (0, -0.13, 0); mirrored "legR", "shinR", "footR" at x = -0.055
  Arms rest rotated 0.25 rad outward (armL.rotation.z = +0.25, armR.rotation.z = -0.25).

FACE: painted, flat, on ONE gently curved low-poly face patch that sits 0.004 in front of the head's front facets (or paint directly into a CanvasTexture mapped on the head's front faces with correct UVs). 1024 x 1024 canvas. Large anime eyes set BELOW the middle of the head: each about 0.085 wide and 0.095 tall, centres 0.085 either side of the middle, 0.15 above the head pivot.
