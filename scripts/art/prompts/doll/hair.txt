SHARED DOLL SPEC (four modules are being written separately to this one spec; they must fit together exactly)

The character is a stylised ball-jointed doll with chibi proportions, 1.00 m tall, about 3.2 heads. Units metres, Y up, facing +z, origin on the floor between the feet. Left is +x.

Skeleton: a hierarchy of THREE.Group pivots. Each pivot's origin is at its joint. Names and rest positions in the parent's space:

        - "head" at (0, 0.05, 0)                   world y 0.69 is the base of the skull. The head is an egg 0.30 wide, 0.31 tall, 0.29 deep; its centre is 0.14 above this pivot; chin at world y 0.675, crown at 0.985
      - "armL" at (0.105, 0.135, 0), "armR" at (-0.105, 0.135, 0)      shoulder joints (world y 0.615). Upper arm hangs down 0.12 long, radius 0.026 tapering to 0.022

Body parameters: bodyWidth in [0.8, 1.3] scales every half-width, depth and limb radius (not lengths, not the head); bodyHeight in [0.9, 1.1] scales limb and torso lengths (not the head). Every module receives the same opts and must apply them the same way.

GENERAL RULES FOR EVERY MODULE
- A plain ES module. No imports, no globals: THREE and kit arrive as arguments and are passed on to helpers. No document, window, fetch or storage.
- kit.canvasTexture(width, height, (ctx, w, h) => {...}) returns a THREE.CanvasTexture; kit.noise(x, y) returns smooth noise in [0, 1].
- Quality bar: a collectible vinyl or resin doll photographed in soft light. Smooth, deliberate forms; no visible faceting (use at least 32 radial segments on every round form and computeVertexNormals after any displacement); no z-fighting (offset shells by at least 0.004).
- MeshPhysicalMaterial throughout. Give every themable mesh userData.slot.
- Return only the code of the module in one ```js block.


YOUR MODULE: hair

Write the HAIR module of the doll. The current hair looks like a hard helmet; this one must read as soft hair made of strands.

export function buildHair(THREE, opts, kit) -> THREE.Group to be added to the "head" pivot (head egg 0.30 x 0.31 x 0.29 centred 0.14 above the pivot; the face is toward +z; the shoulders are 0.075 below the pivot and 0.085 to each side, so long hair must flow around the neck and over or behind the shoulders, not through them).
opts = { hairStyle: "longStraight" | "longWavy" | "bob" | "shortLayered" | "pixie" | "ponytail" | "twinTails" | "bun" | "curlyMedium" | "buzz", hairColor, highlight (lighter colour), fringe: "full" | "sideSwept" | "curtain" | "none" }.

Method:
- A thin scalp cap (the head egg scaled 1.02, covering the hair area only, with a natural hairline) in the hair colour so no skin shows between strands.
- Then hundreds of STRAND RIBBONS grouped in locks. Each lock follows a guide curve (CatmullRomCurve3) that starts at a root on the scalp along a parting line or crown whorl, flows over the skull, and falls with gravity; a lock is 5 to 9 ribbons offset slightly around the guide with small random differences in length and curl. Each ribbon is a curved strip 0.006 to 0.014 wide that tapers to a point at the tip, slightly cupped across its width, built ring by ring as BufferGeometry with correct normals, double-sided.
- Layering: under layer (back, longest), middle layer (sides), top layer (crown), and the fringe locks last, each layer offset outward so they overlap like real hair. At least 28 locks for long styles, 18 for short ones; the fringe has 7 to 11 locks that end in separated points above the eyes.
- Tips must separate and vary; the silhouette must have flyaways and gaps at the ends, not a solid edge. Wavy and curly styles bend the guide curves sinusoidally with phase differences between locks.
- Material: one shared MeshPhysicalMaterial with a painted strand texture (many fine lengthwise lines of slightly varied brightness, darker at the roots, lighter toward the highlight band), sheen 1.0, anisotropy if available, roughness 0.45, and an alpha gradient at the last 15 percent of each ribbon (alphaMap along the length, alphaTest 0.3) so tips look fine.
- Ties and accessories where the style needs them: an elastic for the ponytail, two small ribbons for twin tails, a few pins for the bun.
export function sway(group, t, amount): soft motion, the lower part of each lock moves more than the root (rotate each lock group a little about its root with a per-lock phase), so the hair is visibly not rigid.
