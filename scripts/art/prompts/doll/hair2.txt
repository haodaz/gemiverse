SHARED DOLL SPEC (four modules are being written separately to this one spec; they must fit together exactly)

The character is a stylised ball-jointed doll with chibi proportions, 1.00 m tall, about 3.2 heads. Units metres, Y up, facing +z, origin on the floor between the feet. Left is +x.

Skeleton: a hierarchy of THREE.Group pivots. Each pivot's origin is at its joint. Names and rest positions in the parent's space:
- "root" at (0, 0, 0)
  - "pelvis" at (0, 0.385, 0)                      the hip block, from y 0.38 to 0.48 in world space, hip half-width 0.080, depth 0.075
    - "chest" at (0, 0.095, 0)                     the waist joint (world y 0.48). Chest runs up to the shoulder line at world y 0.62; waist half-width 0.058, shoulder half-width 0.085, depth 0.072
      - "neck" at (0, 0.16, 0)                     world y 0.64; neck radius 0.028, length 0.05
        - "head" at (0, 0.05, 0)                   world y 0.69 is the base of the skull. The head is an egg 0.30 wide, 0.31 tall, 0.29 deep; its centre is 0.14 above this pivot; chin at world y 0.675, crown at 0.985
      - "armL" at (0.105, 0.135, 0), "armR" at (-0.105, 0.135, 0)      shoulder joints (world y 0.615). Upper arm hangs down 0.12 long, radius 0.026 tapering to 0.022
        - "forearmL" / "forearmR" at (0, -0.12, 0)                    elbow. Forearm 0.11 long, radius 0.022 tapering to 0.017
          - "handL" / "handR" at (0, -0.11, 0)                        wrist. Hand 0.055 long
    - "legL" at (0.045, 0, 0), "legR" at (-0.045, 0, 0)               hip joints (world y 0.385). Thigh 0.16 long, radius 0.042 tapering to 0.032
      - "shinL" / "shinR" at (0, -0.16, 0)                            knee. Shin 0.16 long, radius 0.032 tapering to 0.022
        - "footL" / "footR" at (0, -0.16, 0)                          ankle (world y 0.065). Foot 0.085 long toward +z, 0.04 high, 0.04 wide
Arms rest rotated 8 degrees outward (armL.rotation.z = +0.14, armR.rotation.z = -0.14).
Ball joints (visible spheres, a feature of the doll): shoulder r 0.028, elbow r 0.022, wrist r 0.016, hip r 0.040, knee r 0.030, ankle r 0.020, waist r 0.050 (mostly hidden), neck r 0.026.
Body parameters: bodyWidth in [0.8, 1.3] scales every half-width, depth and limb radius (not lengths, not the head); bodyHeight in [0.9, 1.1] scales limb and torso lengths (not the head). Every module receives the same opts and must apply them the same way.

GENERAL RULES FOR EVERY MODULE
- A plain ES module. No imports, no globals: THREE and kit arrive as arguments and are passed on to helpers. No document, window, fetch or storage.
- kit.canvasTexture(width, height, (ctx, w, h) => {...}) returns a THREE.CanvasTexture; kit.noise(x, y) returns smooth noise in [0, 1].
- Quality bar: a collectible vinyl or resin doll photographed in soft light. Smooth, deliberate forms; no visible faceting (use at least 32 radial segments on every round form and computeVertexNormals after any displacement); no z-fighting (offset shells by at least 0.004).
- MeshPhysicalMaterial throughout. Give every themable mesh userData.slot.
- Return only the code of the module in one ```js block.


YOUR MODULE: HAIR (second attempt)

The first attempt was wrong in these ways, so you avoid them: the strands were hair-thin wires with a hard glossy highlight, so it looked like steel wool; a glossy dark scalp cap showed through; a tuft stood straight up at the crown; long hair hung straight through the shoulders. This attempt must read as soft, thick, stylised hair like a good anime figure: big clean CHUNKS with pointed tips, not wires.

export function buildHair(THREE, opts, kit) -> THREE.Group added to the "head" pivot (head egg 0.30 wide, 0.31 tall, 0.29 deep, centred 0.14 above the pivot, face toward +z; shoulders are 0.075 below the pivot and 0.085 to each side).
opts = { hairStyle: "longStraight" | "longWavy" | "bob" | "shortLayered" | "pixie" | "ponytail" | "twinTails" | "bun" | "curlyMedium" | "buzz", hairColor, highlight, fringe: "full" | "sideSwept" | "curtain" | "none" }.

Method:
- A full hair VOLUME shell first: the head ellipsoid scaled about 1.07 to 1.10 (thicker at the top and back, 0.012 to 0.02 of hair thickness), cut along a natural hairline (above the brow in front, behind the ears, down to the nape), in the hair colour, matte. This is the body of the hair and guarantees no scalp shows.
- Then CHUNK LOCKS: each lock is a solid tapered volume (a flattened tube built ring by ring along a CatmullRomCurve3, elliptical cross-section about 0.035 to 0.06 wide and 0.012 to 0.02 thick at the root, tapering smoothly to a sharp point at the tip), slightly curved, overlapping its neighbours like roof tiles so they merge into one mass with a serrated, pointed lower edge. 14 to 22 locks for the back and sides, 5 to 9 for the fringe. Roots start INSIDE the volume shell near a parting line (never a visible bundle or tuft at the crown) and follow the skull closely before falling.
- Fringe locks lie flat on the forehead and end in points just above the eyes (the eye line is 0.125 above the pivot; fringe tips stop at about 0.165), with small gaps between them.
- Long styles flow down the BACK and in front of or behind the shoulders, bending outward over them (keep every point at least 0.012 outside a capsule from (-0.105, -0.07, 0) to (0.105, -0.07, 0) of radius 0.04, and outside the neck).
- Curly and wavy styles use fat spiralling or S-shaped chunks, not thin coils.
- Accessories only where needed (elastic, two ribbons, bun pins), small.
- Material: MeshPhysicalMaterial, roughness 0.7, sheen 0.6 with sheenColor = opts.highlight, NO clearcoat, no strong specular. Paint one soft, wide, horizontal highlight band (an "angel ring") in a texture that wraps the upper third of the hair, in opts.highlight at low contrast, plus very faint lengthwise streaks. Smooth normals (computeVertexNormals).
export function sway(group, t, amount): each chunk lock rotates a little about its root with a per-lock phase, tips moving most, amplitude small (about 2 to 4 degrees).
Keep it under 900 lines. Follow the GENERAL RULES above.
