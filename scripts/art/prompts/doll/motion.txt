SHARED DOLL SPEC (four modules are being written separately to this one spec; they must fit together exactly)

The character is a stylised ball-jointed doll with chibi proportions, 1.00 m tall, about 3.2 heads. Units metres, Y up, facing +z, origin on the floor between the feet. Left is +x.

Skeleton: a hierarchy of THREE.Group pivots. Each pivot's origin is at its joint. Names and rest positions in the parent's space:
- "root" at (0, 0, 0)
  - "pelvis" at (0, 0.385, 0)                      the hip block, from y 0.38 to 0.48 in world space, hip half-width 0.080, depth 0.075
    - "chest" at (0, 0.095, 0)                     the waist joint (world y 0.48). Chest runs up to the shoulder line at world y 0.62; waist half-width 0.058, shoulder half-width 0.085, depth 0.072
      - "neck" at (0, 0.16, 0)                     world y 0.64; neck radius 0.028, length 0.05
        - "head" at (0, 0.05, 0)                   world y 0.69 is the base of the skull. The head is an egg 0.30 wide, 0.31 tall, 0.29 deep; its centre is 0.14 above this pivot; chin at world y 0.675, crown at 0.985
      - "armL" at (0.105, 0.135, 0), "armR" at (-0.105, 0.135, 0)      shoulder joints (world y 0.615). Upper arm hangs down 0.12 long, radius 0.026 tapering to 0.022
        - "forearmL" / "forearmR" at (0, -0.12, 0)                    elbow. Forearm 0.11 long, radius 0.022 tapering to 0.017
          - "handL" / "handR" at (0, -0.11, 0)                        wrist. Hand 0.055 long
    - "legL" at (0.045, 0, 0), "legR" at (-0.045, 0, 0)               hip joints (world y 0.385). Thigh 0.16 long, radius 0.042 tapering to 0.032
      - "shinL" / "shinR" at (0, -0.16, 0)                            knee. Shin 0.16 long, radius 0.032 tapering to 0.022
        - "footL" / "footR" at (0, -0.16, 0)                          ankle (world y 0.065). Foot 0.085 long toward +z, 0.04 high, 0.04 wide
Arms rest rotated 8 degrees outward (armL.rotation.z = +0.14, armR.rotation.z = -0.14).
Ball joints (visible spheres, a feature of the doll): shoulder r 0.028, elbow r 0.022, wrist r 0.016, hip r 0.040, knee r 0.030, ankle r 0.020, waist r 0.050 (mostly hidden), neck r 0.026.
Body parameters: bodyWidth in [0.8, 1.3] scales every half-width, depth and limb radius (not lengths, not the head); bodyHeight in [0.9, 1.1] scales limb and torso lengths (not the head). Every module receives the same opts and must apply them the same way.

GENERAL RULES FOR EVERY MODULE
- A plain ES module. No imports, no globals: THREE and kit arrive as arguments and are passed on to helpers. No document, window, fetch or storage.
- kit.canvasTexture(width, height, (ctx, w, h) => {...}) returns a THREE.CanvasTexture; kit.noise(x, y) returns smooth noise in [0, 1].
- Quality bar: a collectible vinyl or resin doll photographed in soft light. Smooth, deliberate forms; no visible faceting (use at least 32 radial segments on every round form and computeVertexNormals after any displacement); no z-fighting (offset shells by at least 0.004).
- MeshPhysicalMaterial throughout. Give every themable mesh userData.slot.
- Return only the code of the module in one ```js block.


YOUR MODULE: MOTION

Write the motion module for this doll rig. It animates only by rotating (and for the root and pelvis, translating) the named pivots; it never creates geometry. A host calls it every frame.

Design:
- A POSE is a plain object: { pelvisY: number (offset from rest), root: { y?: number, rx?: number }, joints: { [pivotName]: [rx, ry, rz] } } with rotations in radians relative to the rest pose (remember the arms' rest rotation.z of +-0.14 is added by the host; give rotations relative to rest).
- export const CLIPS: an object of clips. Each clip is { loop: boolean, duration: seconds, sample(t) -> POSE } where t is seconds since the clip began.
- export function createAnimator(root): finds the pivots once (root.getObjectByName) and remembers their rest transforms. Returns { play(name, { fade = 0.25 } = {}), update(dt), current(), isDone() }. play cross-fades from the present pose to the new clip over fade seconds by interpolating every joint; update(dt) advances time and writes rotations and positions to the pivots.
- export function walkSpeed(clipName): metres per second the host should move the character while a locomotion clip plays, so feet do not slide.

Clips to write, each with believable human mechanics (this replaces a character that hopped like a zombie, so gait quality matters most):
- "idle": breathing (chest rx), slow weight shift between legs, small head turns, occasional look-around.
- "walk": a real walk cycle, 1.0 s per two steps. Legs alternate with opposite phase: hip swings forward and back about 0.5 rad, the knee bends up to 1.0 rad during swing and is nearly straight at heel strike and through stance, the ankle flexes so the foot lands heel first and pushes off the toe. Arms swing opposite to the legs (about 0.45 rad at the shoulder) with elbows bending slightly more on the forward swing. Pelvis bobs down at double support and up at mid-stance (about 0.012 m), rocks side to side over the stance leg and rotates slightly with the stride; chest counter-rotates; head stays level.
- "run": faster, with a flight phase and more arm bend.
- "turn": a small in-place step.
- "sitDown" (not looping) and "sitIdle": lowers the pelvis to a seat height of 0.20 m with hips flexed about 1.5 rad and knees about 1.5 rad the other way so shins hang vertical, hands resting on thighs; "standUp" reverses it.
- "squat" and "squatIdle": deep knee bend with heels down, arms forward for balance or hands on knees.
- "lieDown" and "lieIdle": goes from standing to sitting to lying on the back (the root rotates to horizontal and lowers so the back rests at a bed height given by the host through options), one arm behind the head; "getUp" reverses.
- "wave": raises the right arm, forearm waving side to side at the elbow, head tilted, a little bounce.
- "patHead": raises one hand to the top of its own head and pats twice.
- "think": one hand to the chin, weight on one leg, head tilted, looks up.
- "nod", "shakeHead", "shrug", "cheer" (both arms up, small jump), "stretch" (arms overhead, back arch), "yawn".
- "reachShelf": steps the weight forward, raises one arm high to a shelf at about 0.75 m, closes the hand, brings the arm down holding an object in front of the chest, looks at it. Export the times at which the hand grasps and releases as CLIPS.reachShelf.events = { grasp, release } so the host can attach a book to the "handR" pivot.
- "putBack": the reverse.
- "readBook": holds both hands in front as if holding an open book, head down, occasional page turn with one hand.
- "writeAtDesk": used while seated: right hand moves in small strokes over a desk at 0.42 m height in front, left hand holds the page, head down, pauses to think now and then.
- "typeAtDesk": both hands in front, fingers implied by small alternating wrist motion.
- "pickUpFromFloor": squat, one hand down to the floor, stand with the object.
- "carry": walk variant with both forearms held up in front as if carrying a box.
- "openDoor": reach forward at handle height, pull, step through.
- "sleep": lying idle with very slow breathing.

Use small helper functions for easing (smoothstep, ease in and out), for mirroring left and right, and for building a pose from a few named angles. Every clip must start and end near the neutral standing pose unless it is an idle of another posture, so that cross-fades look natural. No imports, no globals.
