SHARED DOLL SPEC (four modules are being written separately to this one spec; they must fit together exactly)

The character is a stylised ball-jointed doll with chibi proportions, 1.00 m tall, about 3.2 heads. Units metres, Y up, facing +z, origin on the floor between the feet. Left is +x.

Skeleton: a hierarchy of THREE.Group pivots. Each pivot's origin is at its joint. Names and rest positions in the parent's space:
- "root" at (0, 0, 0)
  - "pelvis" at (0, 0.385, 0)                      the hip block, from y 0.38 to 0.48 in world space, hip half-width 0.080, depth 0.075
    - "chest" at (0, 0.095, 0)                     the waist joint (world y 0.48). Chest runs up to the shoulder line at world y 0.62; waist half-width 0.058, shoulder half-width 0.085, depth 0.072
      - "neck" at (0, 0.16, 0)                     world y 0.64; neck radius 0.028, length 0.05
        - "head" at (0, 0.05, 0)                   world y 0.69 is the base of the skull. The head is an egg 0.30 wide, 0.31 tall, 0.29 deep; its centre is 0.14 above this pivot; chin at world y 0.675, crown at 0.985
      - "armL" at (0.105, 0.135, 0), "armR" at (-0.105, 0.135, 0)      shoulder joints (world y 0.615). Upper arm hangs down 0.12 long, radius 0.026 tapering to 0.022
        - "forearmL" / "forearmR" at (0, -0.12, 0)                    elbow. Forearm 0.11 long, radius 0.022 tapering to 0.017
          - "handL" / "handR" at (0, -0.11, 0)                        wrist. Hand 0.055 long
    - "legL" at (0.045, 0, 0), "legR" at (-0.045, 0, 0)               hip joints (world y 0.385). Thigh 0.16 long, radius 0.042 tapering to 0.032
      - "shinL" / "shinR" at (0, -0.16, 0)                            knee. Shin 0.16 long, radius 0.032 tapering to 0.022
        - "footL" / "footR" at (0, -0.16, 0)                          ankle (world y 0.065). Foot 0.085 long toward +z, 0.04 high, 0.04 wide
Arms rest rotated 8 degrees outward (armL.rotation.z = +0.14, armR.rotation.z = -0.14).
Ball joints (visible spheres, a feature of the doll): shoulder r 0.028, elbow r 0.022, wrist r 0.016, hip r 0.040, knee r 0.030, ankle r 0.020, waist r 0.050 (mostly hidden), neck r 0.026.
Body parameters: bodyWidth in [0.8, 1.3] scales every half-width, depth and limb radius (not lengths, not the head); bodyHeight in [0.9, 1.1] scales limb and torso lengths (not the head). Every module receives the same opts and must apply them the same way.

GENERAL RULES FOR EVERY MODULE
- A plain ES module. No imports, no globals: THREE and kit arrive as arguments and are passed on to helpers. No document, window, fetch or storage.
- kit.canvasTexture(width, height, (ctx, w, h) => {...}) returns a THREE.CanvasTexture; kit.noise(x, y) returns smooth noise in [0, 1].
- Quality bar: a collectible vinyl or resin doll photographed in soft light. Smooth, deliberate forms; no visible faceting (use at least 32 radial segments on every round form and computeVertexNormals after any displacement); no z-fighting (offset shells by at least 0.004).
- MeshPhysicalMaterial throughout. Give every themable mesh userData.slot.
- Return only the code of the module in one ```js block.


YOUR MODULE: clothes

Write the CLOTHES module of the doll. Clothes are real garments with thickness and shape that sit off the body, not paint on the skin.

export function dress(THREE, rig, opts, kit): rig is the body's root group; find pivots with rig.getObjectByName(name) and ADD each garment piece to the pivot it belongs to (sleeves to "armL"/"forearmL", trouser legs to "legL"/"shinL", bodice to "chest", waistband and skirt to "pelvis", shoes to "footL"/"footR"), so clothes move with the limbs. Returns a list of the meshes it added. Apply bodyWidth and bodyHeight exactly as the spec says so garments fit.
opts = { top: "hoodie" | "cardigan" | "sweater" | "shirt" | "tee" | "blazer", topColor, bottom: "pleatedSkirt" | "longSkirt" | "trousers" | "jeans" | "shorts", bottomColor, shoes: "sneakers" | "maryJanes" | "boots" | "loafers", shoeColor, socks: "none" | "ankle" | "knee", sockColor, accent, bodyWidth, bodyHeight }.

Construction rules:
- Every garment is a closed shell with visible thickness (inner and outer wall joined at the hem by a rolled edge of radius about 0.004), offset 0.006 to 0.012 from the body, slightly looser at hems and cuffs so there is a gap and a shadow between cloth and skin.
- Shape comes from cut and gravity: a pleated skirt is a lathe whose radius is modulated around the circumference by a folded-pleat profile (sharp fold, flat panel) deepening toward the hem, flaring to 2.3 times the waist radius, with the hem slightly wavy; a long skirt hangs in soft vertical folds; trousers are two tubes joined at a crotch seam with a waistband, belt loops, a fly line, front creases, and horizontal compression folds behind the knee and at the ankle; shorts have a rolled cuff.
- Tops: a bodice shell with shoulder seams, armholes, and a hem; set-in sleeves as separate tubes with folds at the elbow (three or four ring-shaped wrinkles made by displacing the tube) and ribbed cuffs; collars as bent bezier extrusions that sit on the neck; a hoodie has a soft hood resting on the back and shoulders made as a draped open shell, a kangaroo pocket as a raised patch with openings, drawstrings as thin tubes with aglets; a cardigan is open at the front over a tee with a button band and buttons; a blazer has lapels and a single button.
- Fabric: painted textures per garment type (knit ribs for sweaters and cuffs, denim twill with stitched seams for jeans, fine weave for shirts), used as map and bumpMap; roughness 0.9, sheen 0.5.
- Shoes: a sole with a welt, a shaped upper, toe cap; sneakers with a tongue, eyelets and laces; mary janes with a strap and a small buckle and a glossy clearcoat; boots with a shaft and a pull tab. Socks as thin shells with a ribbed top.
