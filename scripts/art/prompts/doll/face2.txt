SHARED DOLL SPEC (four modules are being written separately to this one spec; they must fit together exactly)

The character is a stylised ball-jointed doll with chibi proportions, 1.00 m tall, about 3.2 heads. Units metres, Y up, facing +z, origin on the floor between the feet. Left is +x.

Skeleton: a hierarchy of THREE.Group pivots. Each pivot's origin is at its joint. Names and rest positions in the parent's space:
- "root" at (0, 0, 0)
  - "pelvis" at (0, 0.385, 0)                      the hip block, from y 0.38 to 0.48 in world space, hip half-width 0.080, depth 0.075
    - "chest" at (0, 0.095, 0)                     the waist joint (world y 0.48). Chest runs up to the shoulder line at world y 0.62; waist half-width 0.058, shoulder half-width 0.085, depth 0.072
      - "neck" at (0, 0.16, 0)                     world y 0.64; neck radius 0.028, length 0.05
        - "head" at (0, 0.05, 0)                   world y 0.69 is the base of the skull. The head is an egg 0.30 wide, 0.31 tall, 0.29 deep; its centre is 0.14 above this pivot; chin at world y 0.675, crown at 0.985
      - "armL" at (0.105, 0.135, 0), "armR" at (-0.105, 0.135, 0)      shoulder joints (world y 0.615). Upper arm hangs down 0.12 long, radius 0.026 tapering to 0.022
        - "forearmL" / "forearmR" at (0, -0.12, 0)                    elbow. Forearm 0.11 long, radius 0.022 tapering to 0.017
          - "handL" / "handR" at (0, -0.11, 0)                        wrist. Hand 0.055 long
    - "legL" at (0.045, 0, 0), "legR" at (-0.045, 0, 0)               hip joints (world y 0.385). Thigh 0.16 long, radius 0.042 tapering to 0.032
      - "shinL" / "shinR" at (0, -0.16, 0)                            knee. Shin 0.16 long, radius 0.032 tapering to 0.022
        - "footL" / "footR" at (0, -0.16, 0)                          ankle (world y 0.065). Foot 0.085 long toward +z, 0.04 high, 0.04 wide
Arms rest rotated 8 degrees outward (armL.rotation.z = +0.14, armR.rotation.z = -0.14).
Ball joints (visible spheres, a feature of the doll): shoulder r 0.028, elbow r 0.022, wrist r 0.016, hip r 0.040, knee r 0.030, ankle r 0.020, waist r 0.050 (mostly hidden), neck r 0.026.
Body parameters: bodyWidth in [0.8, 1.3] scales every half-width, depth and limb radius (not lengths, not the head); bodyHeight in [0.9, 1.1] scales limb and torso lengths (not the head). Every module receives the same opts and must apply them the same way.

GENERAL RULES FOR EVERY MODULE
- A plain ES module. No imports, no globals: THREE and kit arrive as arguments and are passed on to helpers. No document, window, fetch or storage.
- kit.canvasTexture(width, height, (ctx, w, h) => {...}) returns a THREE.CanvasTexture; kit.noise(x, y) returns smooth noise in [0, 1].
- Quality bar: a collectible vinyl or resin doll photographed in soft light. Smooth, deliberate forms; no visible faceting (use at least 32 radial segments on every round form and computeVertexNormals after any displacement); no z-fighting (offset shells by at least 0.004).
- MeshPhysicalMaterial throughout. Give every themable mesh userData.slot.
- Return only the code of the module in one ```js block.


YOUR MODULE: FACE (second attempt)

The first attempt frightened the user. What went wrong, so you avoid it: the eyes were small, set high and wide apart, each under a bulging glass dome, with a hard dark ring; the mouth was a tiny dot; the result was an uncanny baby face. This attempt must be CUTE in the way of a well-loved anime chibi figure or a Nendoroid: simple, flat, graphic, warm.

export function buildFace(THREE, opts, kit) -> THREE.Group added to the "head" pivot. The head is an egg 0.30 wide, 0.31 tall, 0.29 deep, centred 0.14 above the pivot, face toward +z.
opts = { eye (iris colour), eyeShape: "round" | "almond" | "droopy" | "upturned", brow: "soft" | "straight" | "arched", browColor, lip, blush: boolean, freckles: boolean, lashes: boolean }.

Rules:
- NO domes, NO spheres, NO raised geometry for the eyes. Everything is paint on ONE curved face patch: a single mesh that is the front section of an ellipsoid matching the head (radii 0.150 x 0.155 x 0.145, scaled by 1.012 so it sits just above the skin), covering about 150 degrees horizontally and from the brow line to below the chin line, with ONE transparent CanvasTexture of 1024 x 1024 (use the patch's own UVs, computed so the texture is not stretched: u follows horizontal angle, v follows height). MeshBasicMaterial-like flat response: use MeshStandardMaterial with roughness 1, metalness 0, transparent true, depthWrite false, polygonOffset on.
- Layout on the face, anime chibi proportions: the eye line sits BELOW the middle of the head (about 0.125 above the head pivot, that is 0.015 below the head centre). Eyes are large: each about 0.070 wide and 0.078 tall, centres 0.062 either side of the middle. The mouth is 0.040 below the eye line; the nose is only a tiny soft dot or short line between them.
- Eye paint: a tall rounded shape. Thick dark upper lid line (the same dark brown, not black) that curves over the top and thickens at the outer corner, with three short lashes if opts.lashes. Iris fills most of the eye: a vertical gradient from a darker top to a lighter, more saturated bottom in opts.eye, a soft darker pupil in the upper middle, a lighter crescent along the bottom of the iris, and TWO clean white highlights (a big round one upper left, a small one lower right). A little white of the eye visible only at the sides. No hard outline around the whole eye, no lower lid line except a faint short stroke.
- Brows: short, soft, slightly above the eyes, thin. Mouth: a small gentle smile drawn as a curved line with rounded ends, about 0.022 wide, in a muted rose-brown; no teeth, no lips volume. Blush: two wide, very soft pink ovals under the outer half of each eye, alpha about 0.35. Freckles: a few small dots over the nose bridge.
- Expressions redraw the single canvas: "awake" (as above), "happy" (eyes become two thick upward arcs like ^ ^, mouth a wider open smile shape filled with a darker rose), "asleep" (two downward arcs, tiny mouth), "thinking" (irises shifted up and to one side, one brow raised, mouth a small flat line), "unsure" (brows tilted up in the middle, mouth a small wavy line).
export function setExpression(group, name); export function blink(group, t) (draw the closed arcs for about 0.12 s every 3 to 5 s, then restore the current expression).
Keep it under 600 lines. Follow the GENERAL RULES above.
